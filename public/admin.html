📊 Financial Overview & Business Results

ℹ️ How to read this page (ranges, sums, averages, forecast)
• Date range (From/To or the quick buttons) filters income by invoice date and expenses by expense date. It controls the financial cards and the Monthly, Cumulative and Forecast charts.
• Student cards & charts (Active Students, Avg Revenue/Student, Collection Rate, Lessons Delivered, Total Students Owed, Course Type, Completion, Validity, Debts) show the current state and ignore the date range.
• Sums: Total Income, Total Expenses, Net Profit, Total Students Owed, Lessons Delivered.
• Averages: Avg Monthly Revenue = Total Income ÷ number of months in range. Avg Revenue/Student = Total Income in range ÷ active students.
• Percentages: Net Profit Margin = Net Profit ÷ Total Income. Collection Rate = total received ÷ total billed to active students.
• Forecast = the average income of the last 3 months in range, projected forward 3 months (dashed line).
• Hover any card or chart and click ✕ to hide it; use Customize widgets to bring hidden ones back.
